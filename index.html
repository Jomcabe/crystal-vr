How to explore

Desktop: drag the scene to rotate; scroll to scale. Touch: drag to rotate; pinch to scale. Buttons also work with keyboard and touch. Reset restores orientation and size, keeping structure and repetition.

Quest: open this page in Meta Quest Browser, choose Enter VR, aim either controller at the floating menu and press the trigger. The menu remains in front of your starting position; turn back to find it.
